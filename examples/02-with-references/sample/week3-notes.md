# Week 3: The cell

- The **mitochondrion** produces most of the cell's ATP through cellular respiration.
- Mitochondria have their own DNA, which is inherited from the mother.
- ATP stands for adenosine triphosphate. It stores energy in its phosphate bonds.
- The **nucleus** holds most of the cell's DNA and controls gene expression.
- **Ribosomes** build proteins by reading mRNA. They are found free in the cytoplasm and on the rough ER.
- Photosynthesis has two stages: the light reactions, then the Calvin cycle.
- The Calvin cycle takes place in the stroma of the chloroplast.
